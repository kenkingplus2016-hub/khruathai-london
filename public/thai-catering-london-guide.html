What types of Thai catering are available in London?

Thai catering can range from individually packed office lunches and meeting meals to canapé receptions, buffet service and multi-course private banquets. Zab Siam offers office catering, event catering and curated Thai dining experiences, allowing the format to match the venue and schedule.

Can Thai catering accommodate vegan and vegetarian guests?

Yes. Thai menus can include vegetable, tofu and plant-based dishes. Ask the caterer to confirm which sauces, garnishes and stocks are vegan, because some traditional recipes may contain fish sauce, shrimp paste, egg or dairy. Zab Siam can discuss vegan and vegetarian options when preparing the menu.

What allergen information should an organiser request?

Share every dietary requirement before confirming the menu and request an allergen summary for the selected dishes. Common allergens in Thai food can include peanuts, tree nuts, soy, gluten, sesame, shellfish, fish and egg. Requirements should be reconfirmed when the final guest count is submitted.

What details are needed for an accurate catering quote?

  • Event date and serving time
  • Venue address or London postcode
  • Expected and minimum guest count
  • Preferred service style and menu
  • Dietary and allergen requirements
  • Staff, crockery, setup and collection needs
  • Target budget and payment schedule

How far in advance should Thai catering be booked?

Availability depends on the event size and menu. Contact the caterer as early as possible for staffed events or bespoke menus. Confirm the final guest count, delivery window, access instructions and dietary list by the deadline stated in the quotation.

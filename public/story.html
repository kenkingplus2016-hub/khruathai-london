The Legacy of Mae Sangwan

The story of "Chor Malee" is deeply rooted in the heritage of our beloved mother, Mae Sangwan. In the old days of Thailand, when cars and motorcycles were a rare luxury reserved for the wealthy, when roads were still made of red dirt, and electricity and tap water had yet to reach the local villages, she began her journey as a dedicated dessert vendor. Every day, she carried her beautifully crafted sweets on a traditional wooden shoulder pole (Haab) and walked miles along the rugged paths to share her passion.

Her dedication to authentic flavors and meticulous craftsmanship became a legend in our hometown. But her quiet dream was always to see the rich, sweet taste of Thailand cherished beyond our borders.

Today, as her proud descendants, we have taken up her legacy and carried it all the way to England. We brought with us not just her secret recipes, but the heart and soul of Thai culture. By blending her traditional, time-honored techniques with a modern premium touch, "Chor Malee" was reborn in the UK.

Every dessert we serve is a tribute to Mae Sangwan's enduring spirit, preserving the rich history, the aroma of fresh coconut, and the sweet nostalgia of the past, proudly presented to you right here in England.
